The goal of the project is to create a "Less-Bad AI" framework that ensures every change made by an AI is tracked in a way that can be undone, maintained, and reasoned about.

The target user is not professional developers with established and enforced agentic workflows but solo and indie developers who tend to take the path of least resistance, use auto mode, and blindly agree to most changes the AI suggests.

The idea is to be a "compiler" of agentic code. It doesn't tell the user what it's doing, or ask how things should be done. There is an efficient and idiomatic way to do things in programming. Let's call this "correct".
